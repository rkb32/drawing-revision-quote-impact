How it works

Inputs: Rev A and Rev B PDFs, BOM, vendor quote sheet. Outputs: an Excel impact sheet, a marked-up PDF, and draft vendor reconfirm emails (drafts only, nothing is sent).

What it can't do yet

It reads about a dozen fields from text, from OCR'd scans, and from rotated sheets. It cross-checks the revision block against what it found, and it lists where each change sits on the sheet. Tested only on synthetic sheets. Real customer drawings are untested. GD&T labels and common symbols are parsed on a synthetic symbol sheet only. A field it can't read is flagged as unchecked, not as "no change". Engineering time and material cost are not priced.
